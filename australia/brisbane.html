    Weather Report For Brisbane

    Welcome to the weather report for Brisbane, in Queensland, Australia (Coordinates: 27.4698°S, 153.0251°E; approximate elevation: 28 m). Brisbane sits on the Brisbane River, a short way inland from Moreton Bay, with the river winding through the city past parks, bridges, and the South Bank. It is the third-largest city in Australia, a warm, outdoor city where people eat outside all year. Brisbane has a subtropical climate, which means hot, humid summers and mild, dry winters. The river and the bay shape the weather, with storms in summer and clear, sunny days in winter. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather Conditions Brisbane

    Weather conditions Brisbane depend on the river, the bay, and the season. In summer, warm, wet air moves in from the sea, and afternoon storms build fast, with heavy rain, lightning, and gusty wind. In winter, dry air takes over, and the days turn sunny and mild, with cool nights. The bay breeze cools the city in the afternoon, most of all near the river. Because Brisbane is low and flat, floods can follow very heavy rain, and the river can rise fast after a big storm. The live card at the top of this page follows all of this hour by hour, with values from the MET Norway model for the exact spot of Brisbane.

    Weather In Brisbane Australia

    The weather in Brisbane Australia in summer is hot and humid, with strong sun and storms. Mornings are often clear and sticky, and by the afternoon, dark cloud builds and rain falls hard for a short time. Heavy rain can flood low streets, so check the forecast before long drives. Nights stay warm, and the air rarely cools much. Drink water, stay in the shade at midday, and plan outdoor work for the morning. The hourly page shows the hottest hours and the timing of storms, so you can plan the day around them.

    Weather Brisbane

    Weather Brisbane in winter is mild, dry, and sunny, with cool nights. Days are often bright and pleasant, good for walks along the river and time in the parks. Rain is rare in winter, and the air feels fresh and clear. Nights can be cold enough for a warm coat, most of all away from the city center, where cold air pools in low spots. Winter is the most settled season in Brisbane, with long runs of fine days. It is a good time to visit, with blue skies and easy weather for time outside.
