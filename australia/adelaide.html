    Adelaide City Weather

    Welcome to the weather report for Adelaide, in South Australia, Australia (Coordinates: 34.9282°S, 138.5999°E). Adelaide sits on a coastal plain between the Gulf St Vincent and the Mount Lofty Ranges, with parklands ringing the city center and the sea a short drive west. It is the capital of South Australia, a city of wide streets, gardens, and festivals. Adelaide has a Mediterranean climate, which means hot, dry summers and cool, wet winters. The gulf and the hills shape the weather, with sea breezes in summer and rain fronts in winter.     Weather Of Adelaide Australia

    The weather of Adelaide Australia in winter is cool and wet, with green parklands and full gardens. Rain comes with cold fronts from the ocean, and it can fall steadily for a day or more.
