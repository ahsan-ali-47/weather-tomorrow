    Australian Perth Weather

    Welcome to the weather report for Perth, in Western Australia, Australia (Coordinates: 31.9505°S, 115.8605°E; approximate elevation: 20 m). Perth sits on the Swan River, a short way from the Indian Ocean, with the port of Fremantle at the river mouth and Kings Park on a hill above the city. It is one of the most remote large cities in the world, closer to Southeast Asia than to Sydney. Perth has a Mediterranean climate, which means hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. The ocean and the river shape the weather, with a famous afternoon sea breeze that cools the city on hot days. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Perth Oz Weather

    Perth Oz weather is ruled by the sea breeze, known as the Fremantle Doctor. On hot days, cool air flows in from the Indian Ocean in the afternoon and drops the temperature fast, most of all near the coast and the river. Mornings can be still and hot, then the breeze arrives and the city breathes again. Oz is a short, fond name for Australia, and Perth is its sunniest large city, with clear skies on most days of the year. The live card at the top of this page follows the breeze hour by hour, with wind speed and direction from the MET Norway model for the exact spot of Perth.

    Perth W A Weather

    Perth W A weather is dry and bright for most of the year. W A is short for Western Australia, the huge state where Perth sits. The city gets most of its rain in winter, when cold fronts sweep in from the ocean. Summers are dry, with weeks of sun and no rain at all. The Swan River adds a little local moisture, and Kings Park catches the breeze above the city. Because the land is flat and open, wind can be strong, most of all near the coast. The 7-day strip on this page shows the dry and wet spells at a glance.

    Perth Wa Australia Weather

    Perth Wa Australia weather in summer is hot and dry, with strong sun and warm nights. Heat can build for days when hot air blows in from the desert interior, and the city can swelter until the sea breeze arrives. The breeze is the key to summer comfort in Perth, and the hourly page shows when it is due each day. Drink water, stay in the shade at midday, and plan walks for the morning or the evening. Bushfire smoke can drift over the city on the hottest, windiest days, so check the forecast and local advice when the air smells of smoke.

    Perth West Australia Weather

    Perth West Australia weather in winter is mild and wet, with green hills and full rivers. Rain comes with cold fronts from the ocean, and it can fall steadily for a day or more. Between fronts, winter days are often bright and pleasant, good for walks in Kings Park and along the river. Nights are cool, and frost is rare near the coast. The city turns green in winter after the dry summer, and the parks fill with people on fine days. A waterproof coat covers most winter days, with an umbrella for the wettest fronts.

    Weather For Perth Western Australia

    The weather for Perth Western Australia in spring is mild and fresh, with wildflowers in the hills and parks around the city. Days warm up, nights stay cool, and the wind can be strong, most of all near the coast. Spring rain eases as summer nears, and the days grow longer and brighter. It is one of the best times to visit, with easy weather for walks, beaches, and the river. The 7-day strip shows the shift from wet to dry, and the hourly page shows the wind hour by hour, which matters for beach plans.

    Weather In Perth Oz

    The weather in Perth Oz in autumn is warm and settled, with cooling nights and the first rains of the season. Days stay sunny well into autumn, and the sea is still warm after summer, so beach days run late in the year. As autumn goes on, cold fronts return, rain becomes more common, and the city greens up again. Autumn suits outdoor life, with mild days and few crowds. The sea breeze still arrives on warm days, though it weakens as the ocean cools. Check the forecast for the first big front of the season.

    Weather Perth Wa Australia

    Weather Perth Wa Australia in a heat wave needs care. Days can stay very hot for a week, with warm nights that give little relief. The sea breeze may arrive late or not at all on the hottest days. Drink water often, stay out of the sun at midday, and check on older people and young children. The hourly page shows the hottest hours, and the 10-day page shows how long the heat may last. Cool places like the river, the coast, and shaded parks help, and the breeze, when it comes, drops the heat fast.

    Western Australia Perth Weather

    Western Australia Perth weather across the year runs from hot, dry summers to mild, wet winters, with two fine seasons in between. Spring is fresh and full of flowers. Autumn is warm and settled. The best weather for most visitors comes in spring and autumn, when days are mild and the air is clear. Summer suits beaches and the river, for people who love heat.
