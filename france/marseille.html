    Weather Forecast for Marseille France

    Welcome to the weather page for Marseille, in Provence, France (Coordinates: 43.2965°N, 5.3698°E; elevation: 12 m). Marseille is a large port city on the Mediterranean Sea, and it is the oldest city in France. Greek traders built it long ago, and ships have come and gone from its old port ever since. The city spreads around a curved bay, with white limestone hills behind it and the rocky inlets of the Calanques to the east. A church with a tall statue stands on a hill above the port and can be seen from far out at sea. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Marseille, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Marseille has a Mediterranean climate. Summers are hot and dry, with strong sun and very little rain. Winters are mild and wet, with most of the year's rain falling in short bursts. The most famous wind here is the Mistral, a strong cold wind from the north-west that can blow for days, clear the sky, and make the air feel sharp. Spring and autumn are often the most pleasant seasons, with warm days and fresh breezes from the sea. The rest of this page explains each season in simple words, and shows how to use the 7-day outlook and the 10-day forecast.

    Marseille Weather

    Marseille weather is shaped by the sea, the hills, and the Mistral wind. The Mediterranean Sea keeps the winters mild, because the water holds warmth from the summer and gives it back slowly. The hills behind the city shelter it from some storms, but they also squeeze extra rain out of wet winds. When the Mistral blows, the sky turns a deep blue, the air dries out, and the wind can be strong enough to make walking hard near the port.     Weather in Marseille

    The weather in Marseille follows the seasons of the Mediterranean. In summer, the sun is strong, the days are long, and rain is rare for weeks at a time. The sea breeze in the afternoon takes the edge off the heat near the port. In winter, the days are short and mild, with rain that comes in fast showers and then clears. Spring brings flowers to the hills and warm days that can still turn cool when the Mistral blows. Autumn is often warm at first, then turns wet, with heavy rain at times when storms move in from the sea.

    Weather of Marseille

    The weather of Marseille has a clear rhythm through the year. The wet season runs from autumn to early spring, when storms from the Atlantic and the Mediterranean bring rain. The dry season runs from late spring to early autumn, when high pressure sits over the region and the sun rules the sky. Even in the dry season, a short storm can build over the hills on a hot afternoon. The hills and the sea make the weather change fast, so a morning forecast can look different by the evening.     Weather in Marseille France

    The weather in Marseille France is kind to people who love the outdoors. The city gets sun on most days of the year, and the mild winters mean that walking, cycling, and sitting outside are possible even in the cold months. The port stays busy all year, and the fish market opens early each morning no matter the season. In summer, the beaches fill up, and the water is warm enough to swim from late spring to mid-autumn. In winter, the city is quieter, the light is soft, and the hills behind the city are green after the rain.

    Marseille Weather by Month

    Marseille weather by month moves from cool and wet to hot and dry and back again. Winter months are mild, with rain and the Mistral. Spring months warm up fast, with flowers on the hills and longer days. Summer months are hot and dry, with strong sun and very little rain. Autumn months start warm, then turn wet, and the first storms can bring heavy rain. The sea is warmest in late summer and early autumn, and coolest at the end of winter. The best months for walking and sitting outside are late spring and early autumn, when the heat is mild and the rain is light.
