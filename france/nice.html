    Weather Forecast in Nice

    Welcome to the weather page for Nice, on the French Riviera, France (Coordinates: 43.7102°N, 7.2620°E; elevation: 10 m). Nice sits on a curved bay called the Bay of Angels, with the sea in front and green hills behind. A wide seaside walk, the Promenade des Anglais, runs along the shore, and the old town with its flower market sits at one end of the bay. The city is close to the border with Italy, and its mild climate has drawn visitors for a very long time. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Nice, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Nice has a mild Mediterranean climate. The sun shines on most days of the year, summers are warm, and winters are mild. The sea keeps the nights soft, and the hills behind the city shelter it from cold north winds. Rain falls mostly in the autumn and winter, often in short heavy showers, while the summer months stay dry. The Mistral wind can reach the city at times, but the hills break its force.     Weather in Nice France

    The weather in Nice France follows the rhythm of the Mediterranean. The dry season runs from late spring to early autumn, with long sunny days and very little rain. The wet season runs from autumn to early spring, with rain that falls in bursts and then clears to bright sun. Spring is full of flowers, and the hills turn green. Autumn starts warm, with a warm sea, and then turns cooler as the storms arrive. Even in the wet season, long spells of sun are common, and the light over the bay stays bright and clear.

    Weather in Nice

    The weather in Nice is shaped by three things: the sea, the hills, and the sun. The sea softens the cold in winter and cools the heat in summer. The hills shelter the city from cold winds and catch extra rain. The sun shines on most days, which keeps the city bright even when it is cool. In the summer, the hottest hours are in the early afternoon, and the sea breeze brings relief by the shore. In the winter, the coldest hours are just before dawn, and the sun soon warms the seafront again.

    Does it rain much in Nice?

    Rain falls mostly in the autumn and winter, often in short heavy showers, while the summer months stay dry. The forecast shows rain in millimetres, so you can see at once how much is due on each day.

    What is the best time to visit Nice?

    Late spring and early autumn are the best times. The days are warm, the sea is pleasant, and the sun shines on most days. Summer is warm and busy, and winter is mild and bright, with rain at times.

    Does Nice get snow?

    Snow is very rare in Nice, and it melts fast when it falls. The sea keeps the city mild, and snow settles only on the hills behind the city, not on the seafront.

    How hot does Nice get in summer?

    Summers in Nice are warm, with strong sun and dry air. A breeze from the sea keeps the seafront pleasant, and the evenings stay warm enough to sit outside late into the night.
