    France Weather Bordeaux

    Welcome to the weather report for Bordeaux, in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France (Coordinates: 44.8412°N, 0.5800°W). Bordeaux sits on the Garonne River, a short way inland from the Atlantic coast, in the heart of one of the most famous wine regions in the world. The river curves through the city past grand stone buildings, wide squares, and the Place de la Bourse on the water. Bordeaux has an oceanic climate, which means mild, wet winters and warm summers, with rain in every season and wind off the ocean. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather Bordeaux France

    Weather Bordeaux France is set by the river and the ocean. The Atlantic lies close to the west, and moist air moves in from the sea all year, bringing cloud and rain. The Garonne River adds fog on calm mornings, most of all in autumn and winter, when cool air sits over water that is still mild. The land around the city is flat and low, with vineyards in every direction, so wind can sweep through with little to stop it.     Weather Bordeaux

    Weather Bordeaux in winter is mild and wet, with wind from the Atlantic and rain on many days. Hard frost is rare, and snow is almost never seen in the city. Grey skies can last for days, but mild spells break through, with sun and soft air. The river can flood after long rain, and the wind can gust hard in a winter storm. A waterproof coat covers most winter days, with layers for the wind. The 7-day strip shows which days look wet, and the hourly page shows when the rain should start and stop.

    Weather In Bordeaux

    The weather in Bordeaux in spring is mild and bright, with longer days and warmer air. Showers pass through often, and the vineyards turn green fast. Blossom appears in the parks, and the river paths fill with walkers on fine days. Mornings can still be cool, and fog can sit over the river until late morning. Layers are the smart choice, because a cold, grey morning can end with a warm, sunny afternoon. Spring is a good time to visit, before the heat of summer and the crowds.

    Bordeaux France September Weather

    Bordeaux France September weather is warm and settled, with harvest time in the vineyards. Days are often sunny and mild, with cool nights, and the air feels like late summer. Rain can return in September, but it rarely lasts all day. The grape harvest fills the country around the city with workers, and the markets overflow with fruit. September is one of the best months to visit, with easy weather for walks, the river, and day trips to the coast. Check the 7-day strip for the driest days.

Mornings bring fog near the river on calm days, which usually lifts by late morning. Rain becomes more common as October goes on, and the first strong winds of the season can arrive from the Atlantic. A warm coat and an umbrella cover most October days.
