    Understanding 10-Day Long-Range Weather Projections

    Long-range 10-day meteorological projections utilize complex mathematical equations of atmospheric physics executed on high-performance supercomputers at the Meteorological Institute of Norway (MET Norway). These models simulate barometric pressure fields, geopotential height contours, and tropospheric moisture gradients across the South Asian subcontinent.


    For agriculture in the Punjab and Sindh river basins, extended outlooks provide crucial guidance for irrigation scheduling, crop harvesting, and fertilizer application. During transition seasons, they assist travelers navigating mountain passes in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and northern highlands.

    10-Day Forecast FAQs

    Atmospheric conditions are nonlinear and dynamic. As new satellite soundings and meteorological observations are recorded each hour, numerical models refine precipitation timing, cloud fraction, and thermal boundaries.
    Models accurately identify moisture advection and rain-bearing frontal passages up to 10 days in advance; localized millimetric rainfall amounts become increasingly precise within 48 to 72 hours of arrival.
