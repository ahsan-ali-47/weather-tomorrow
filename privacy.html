    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 2026 • Your data protection and privacy are our top priority.

    At Weather Tomorrow, accessible from https://www.weathertomorrow.website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Weather Tomorrow and how we utilize it.

    1. Information We Collect

    Weather Tomorrow operates on a minimal-data philosophy. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal identity verification. When you search for a city or location, geographic coordinates are processed strictly in real-time by your browser and our stateless proxy functions to retrieve weather forecasts. We never store personal location profiles.

    2. Log Files & Analytics

    Like most web platforms, Weather Tomorrow uses standard server log files. The information inside log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

    3. Cookies & Local Storage

    We use browser local storage (localStorage) solely to remember your chosen language setting (such as English or Urdu) so you do not need to reselect it upon subsequent page visits. Third-party advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

    4. Third-Party Meteorological Services

    Our site communicates with the Meteorological Institute of Norway (api.met.no) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. All queries are relayed via secure serverless proxies that transmit an identifying User-Agent header with contact details to fulfill public API terms of service.

    5. GDPR & CCPA Data Rights

    Under European GDPR and California CCPA regulations, visitors have the right to request information regarding data collection. Because we do not store personal profiles, databases, or cookies tracking individual identities, we collect zero persistent personal records.

    6. Contact Information

    If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website.
