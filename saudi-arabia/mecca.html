    Weather Forecast for Mecca Saudi Arabia

    Welcome to the weather page for Mecca, in Makkah Province, Saudi Arabia (Coordinates: 21.3891°N, 39.8579°E; elevation: 277 m). Mecca sits in a valley ringed by hills, inland from the Red Sea, in the Hejaz region of western Saudi Arabia. It is the holiest city in Islam, home to the Grand Mosque and the Kaaba, and pilgrims from all over the world come here through the year. The hills around the city are bare and rocky, and the streets are busy day and night. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Mecca, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Mecca has a hot desert climate. Summers are very hot, with strong sun and dry air. Winters are warm, with mild days and cool nights. Rain is very rare, and most years bring only a few short showers, mainly in the winter months. The dry air means the heat feels different from the damp heat of the coast, but the sun is still fierce in the middle of the day. Shade, water, and rest are vital in the hot months. The rest of this page explains each season in simple words, and shows how to use the 7-day outlook and the 10-day forecast.

    Mecca Weather

    Mecca weather is hot for most of the year, and the sun is the force that shapes each day. The city sits in a valley, and the hills trap the heat, so the air can feel still and heavy in the middle of the day. Mornings warm up fast after sunrise, and the heat peaks in the early afternoon. Evenings cool down slowly, and the nights in winter can feel quite cool. The air is dry, which helps sweat dry fast, but it also hides how much water the body loses.     Saudi Mecca Weather

    Saudi Mecca weather fits the pattern of the Hejaz, the western region of Saudi Arabia. The Red Sea coast to the west is hot and damp, while the inland hills and valleys are hot and dry. Mecca sits between the two, with dry air and very little rain. Jeddah, on the coast, feels more damp, and the high town of Taif, up in the hills, feels much cooler. Pilgrims who travel between these places in one day can feel three kinds of weather.
