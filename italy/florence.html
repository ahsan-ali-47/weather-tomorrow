    Weather Forecast Florence Italy

    Welcome to the weather page for Florence, in Tuscany, Italy (Coordinates: 43.7696°N, 11.2558°E; elevation: 50 m). Florence sits on the River Arno, in a valley ringed by green hills, in the heart of Tuscany. It is the city where the Renaissance began, and its great church with the red dome, its old bridge with shops on it, and its art galleries draw visitors from all over the world. The hills around the city are covered with olive trees, vines, and tall cypress trees. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Florence, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Florence has a Mediterranean climate with hot summers and mild winters. Summers are hot and dry, with strong sun and warm nights. Winters are mild and wet, with rain that falls mostly in the autumn and winter months. Spring and autumn are the most pleasant seasons, with warm days, cool nights, and light over the hills that artists have loved for centuries. The rest of this page explains each season in simple words, and shows how to use the 7-day outlook and the 10-day forecast.

    Weather Florence Italy

    Weather Florence Italy is shaped by the valley, the river, and the hills. The valley can trap heat in the summer, and the city can feel very hot when the air stands still. The hills catch clouds and rain, so a shower can fall on the hills while the centre stays dry. The Arno river runs through the city, and its banks are lined with paths and old buildings. In the winter, mist can sit over the river in the morning and lift as the sun rises. The card at the top of this page shows the live temperature, wind, dampness, and pressure for Florence right now.

    Weather in Florence Italy

    The weather in Florence Italy follows the seasons of Tuscany. In summer, the sun is strong, the days are long, and rain is rare for weeks at a time. The stone streets hold the heat, and the coolest places are the shaded gardens and the river banks. In winter, the days are short and mild, with rain that comes in bursts and then clears. Spring brings flowers to the hills and warm days that can still turn cool at night. Autumn is often the finest season, with golden light, ripe grapes, and the first rains after the dry summer.

    Florence Italy Weather

    Florence Italy weather is warmer than the weather in the north of Italy, and drier in the summer. The plains of the north get more fog and more rain, while Florence gets more sun and longer dry spells. The hills of Tuscany make the weather local: one valley can be sunny while the next one catches a storm. That is why a live forecast for the exact spot matters more here than a broad regional outlook. This page uses the MET Norway model for the exact place where Florence stands.

    Florence Weather by Month

    Florence weather by month moves from cool and wet to hot and dry and back again. The winter months are mild, with rain and mist over the river. Spring months warm up fast, with flowers on the hills and longer days. Summer months are hot and dry, with strong sun and warm nights. Autumn months start warm, then turn wet, and the hills turn gold and red. The river is fullest after the autumn rains. The best months for walking and sitting outside are late spring and early autumn, when the heat is mild and the light is at its finest.

    Florence Italy Weather by Month

    Florence Italy weather by month is a good guide for choosing when to visit. The cool months are best for museums and galleries, when the streets are quiet and the light is soft. The warm months are best for gardens, hills, and long evenings outside, when the city stays warm after sunset. The spring months bring flowers and mild days. The autumn months bring harvest time, with grapes and olives, and the first rains. No month is without charm, but the months with mild days and light rain give the easiest walking and the clearest views of the dome and the hills.

    Weather in Florence Italy in April

    The weather in Florence Italy in April is spring at its best. The days grow longer and warmer, the hills turn green, and flowers open in the gardens. Showers can still fall, and the mornings can be cool, so a light jacket is wise. The sun is strong by the middle of the day, and the light over the river and the dome is clear and bright. April is a fine month for walking, because the heat of summer has not yet arrived and the crowds are smaller than in the high season. Check the 7-day outlook before you go, since spring showers can arrive fast.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Florence Weather

    What is the weather in Florence today?

    This page shows the live weather for Florence, with the current temperature, wind, dampness, and pressure, plus a 7-day outlook and a link to the 10-day forecast. The data comes from MET Norway and is updated every hour.

    How accurate is the Florence forecast?

    Days 1 to 3 are generally the most reliable. Days 4 to 10 are ensemble model projections, which means the model is run more than once with small changes. They are a good guide for planning, and they are refreshed every hour.

    Does it rain much in Florence?

    Rain falls mostly in the autumn and winter, often in bursts that clear to sun. The summer months stay dry. The forecast shows rain in millimetres, so you can see at once how much is due on each day.

    What is the best time to visit Florence?

    Late spring and early autumn are the best times. The days are warm, the light is fine, and the hills are green or golden. Summer is hot and busy, and winter is mild but wet at times.

    Does Florence get snow?

    Snow is rare in Florence, and it melts fast when it falls. The winters are mild, and snow settles only on the hills around the city, not in the streets for long.

    How hot does Florence get in summer?

    Summers in Florence are hot and dry, with strong sun and warm nights. The valley can trap the heat, so the stone streets feel very warm in the afternoon. Shade, water, and the cool gardens give relief.
