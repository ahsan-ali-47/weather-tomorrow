    Naples Italy Weather

    Welcome to the weather report for Naples, in Campania, Italy (Coordinates: 40.8359°N, 14.2488°E; approximate elevation: 99.8 m). Naples sits on the north shore of the Bay of Naples, on the Tyrrhenian Sea. The island of Capri lies out in the bay, and Mount Vesuvius rises across the water to the east. Naples is one of the oldest cities in Italy. It is a busy port, with narrow old streets, wide roads along the sea, and hills that rise steeply behind the center. The sea shapes the weather here. It keeps winters mild, and it brings a cool breeze on hot summer days. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather In Naples Italy

    Naples sits between the sea and the hills, and that mix sets the tone for weather in Naples Italy all year. The bay holds warmth, so cold snaps are short and snow in the streets by the sea is rare. In summer, a breeze off the water cools the sea-front in the afternoon, while the hills behind the city stay warmer for longer. When wind blows in from the sea, cloud and rain can arrive fast, and a clear morning can turn grey by lunch. The hills can also trap cloud over the city, so one part of Naples can be wet while another stays dry. This is why the live forecast matters more than a fixed idea of the season. Check the card at the top of this page before you go out, and check the hourly page when rain is near.

    December Weather In Naples Italy

    December weather in Naples Italy is cool and mild for winter, with rain on many days. Days are short, and the sun sets early over the bay. Daytime air is often cool enough for a warm coat, and nights are cold, but hard frost is rare near the sea. Rain can be heavy at times, and wind off the water can make it feel colder than the number on the card. The sea is still mild after the warm months, which softens the coldest nights. Clear days in December are bright and fine, with sharp views across the bay to Capri and Vesuvius. Check the hourly page in the morning, because a wet start can clear by the afternoon.

    Naples Italy February Weather

    Naples Italy February weather is the coldest part of the year, but it stays mild next to most of Europe. Rain and wind are common, and grey days can run together for a week. Still, mild spells break through, with sun and soft air from the sea. The hills around the city can catch cloud while the sea-front stays clear, or the reverse. Late in the month, the first signs of spring appear, with longer days and buds on the trees in the parks. Snow is very rare in the city streets, though the high ground inland can see a light dusting. A warm coat and an umbrella cover most February days in Naples.

    Naples Weather Italy

    Naples weather Italy in summer is hot and bright, with strong sun and warm nights. The sea breeze helps in the afternoon, most of all along the sea-front and in the open squares. Inland streets and the hills can feel hotter, because the air moves less there. Humidity rises when the wind comes off the warm sea, so hot days can feel sticky. Thunderstorms build at times, with short, heavy rain that clears fast. Drink water, stay in the shade at midday, and plan walks for the morning or the evening.     Naples Italy Weather January

    Naples Italy weather January brings the heart of winter. Days are cool, nights are cold, and rain is the main story. Wind from the sea adds a chill, and long spells of cloud are normal. Even so, January has fine, bright days too, when the sun shines and the bay looks clear and blue. The sea keeps the worst cold away, so the air rarely drops far below freezing near the shore. Dress in layers, keep a coat that blocks wind, and carry an umbrella. The 7-day strip shows which days look wet, and the hourly page shows when the rain is due to start and stop.

    Naples Italy Weather March

    Naples Italy weather March marks the start of spring. Days get longer and warmer, and the sun gains strength. Mornings can still be cold, and showers pass through often, but the city turns green and the parks fill with people on fine days. The sea is still cool after winter, so the breeze can feel fresh even on a warm afternoon. Wind can change fast in March, and a calm morning can end with a wet evening. Layers are the smart choice, with a light coat for the evening. March is a good month for walks, because the heat of summer has not arrived yet.

    January Weather In Naples Italy

    January weather in Naples Italy means rain more than cold. Most winter rain comes from low pressure systems that move in from the sea, and it can fall as steady light rain or as short, hard showers. The hills around the city wring extra rain out of the passing air, so totals can differ from one part of Naples to another. The forecast on this page shows rain in millimetres, which is the amount the model expects, not a vague chance. Check the hourly page to see which hours look wet. An umbrella and shoes that cope with wet streets are all most January days need.

    Naples In March Weather Italy

    Naples in March weather Italy is kind to trips out of the city, with one caution: the sea. Ferries to Capri and Ischia run in most weather, but strong wind and rough seas can change the plan, so check the forecast on the morning you travel. On land, March suits day trips to the hills and the coast, with mild air and fewer crowds than summer. Rain showers can still appear, so keep a light waterproof layer in your bag.
