    Weather Forecast Frankfurt

    Welcome to the weather page for Frankfurt, in Hesse, Germany (Coordinates: 50.1109°N, 8.6821°E; elevation: 112 m). Frankfurt stands on the River Main, and its full name, Frankfurt am Main, tells you which river it stands on. It is the money centre of Germany, home to the European Central Bank, and its tall glass towers have earned it the name Mainhattan. The old town, with the Römer town hall, sits near the river, and the city hosts some of the largest trade fairs in the world. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Frankfurt, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Frankfurt has a temperate climate, which means no season is extreme, but the weather can change fast. Summers are warm, winters are cold, and rain can fall in any month of the year. Winds from the west bring mild and damp air from the Atlantic, while winds from the east can bring cold air in winter and hot air in summer. Spring and autumn are changeable, with warm days and cool nights. The rest of this page explains each season in simple words, and shows how to use the 7-day outlook and the 10-day forecast.

    Frankfurt Weather

    Frankfurt weather is changeable, and a sunny morning can turn to rain by the afternoon. The city sits in a river valley, which can trap damp air and fog in the cold months. In the warm months, the valley can also trap heat, and the tall towers make the centre feel warmer than the parks and suburbs. The River Main adds dampness to the air, and the morning mist over the river is a common sight in autumn.     Frankfurt Germany Weather

    Frankfurt Germany weather is mild compared with the rest of the country. The south-west of Germany is warmer than the north and the east, and Frankfurt sits in one of the warmest parts. Winters here are cold, but not as harsh as in the hills to the east or the far north. Summers are warm, with long days and storms that build on hot afternoons. Rain falls through the year, with no truly dry season.     Weather Conditions in Frankfurt

    The weather conditions in Frankfurt change with the seasons and with the wind. West winds bring mild, damp air and most of the rain. East winds bring drier air that is cold in winter and hot in summer. North winds bring cool air, and south winds bring warm air. In winter, fog and low cloud can sit over the river valley for days, and the sun may not break through. In summer, hot days can end with strong storms that bring heavy rain and lightning.     Weather in Frankfurt Germany

    The weather in Frankfurt Germany follows the four seasons in a clear way. Spring brings longer days, flowers in the parks, and days that can be warm or cool. Summer brings warm days, long evenings, and storms on hot afternoons. Autumn brings golden leaves, mist over the river, and the first cold nights. Winter brings short days, frost, and snow that comes and goes. Each season has its own charm, and the city stays busy through all of them, with trade fairs, markets, and festivals in every month of the year.

    Weather Frankfurt am Main Germany

    Weather Frankfurt am Main Germany is the full name of the city's weather, and the river is part of the story. The Main flows through the city from east to west, and its banks are lined with paths, parks, and museums. On warm days, people sit by the river and ride boats on the water. On cold days, mist rises from the river in the morning. The river keeps the air a little damp, which can make cold days feel colder and warm days feel warmer. The bridges over the river give fine views of the towers, and they are a good place to feel the wind and see the sky.

    Weather in Frankfurt

    The weather in Frankfurt is kind to people who like the outdoors, as long as they dress for change. A light rain jacket is wise in every season, because showers can arrive fast. In the summer, light clothes and sun cream are needed, and shade is easy to find in the parks. In the winter, a warm coat, gloves, and a hat are needed, and the Christmas market is best enjoyed with warm drinks. The city's parks, the river paths, and the forest to the south are good in all seasons, and each one looks different as the year turns.

    Frankfurt Weather by Month

    Frankfurt weather by month moves from cold winter to warm summer and back. The winter months are cold, with frost, fog, and snow at times. Spring months warm up slowly, with more sun and more showers. Summer months are warm, with long days and storms on hot afternoons. Autumn months turn cool and misty, with golden leaves and the first frosts. Rain falls in all months, and no month is truly dry.     Frequently Asked Questions About Frankfurt Weather

    What is the weather in Frankfurt today?

    How accurate is the Frankfurt forecast?

    Days 1 to 3 are generally the most reliable. Days 4 to 10 are ensemble model projections, which means the model is run more than once with small changes. They are a good guide for planning, and they are refreshed every hour.

    Does it rain much in Frankfurt?

    Rain falls through the year in Frankfurt, and no month is truly dry. The forecast shows rain in millimetres, so you can see at once how much is due. Showers can arrive fast, so a light rain jacket is wise in every season.

    What is the best time to visit Frankfurt?

    Late spring and early autumn are the best times. The days are mild, the parks are green or golden, and the light is soft. Summer is warm and busy, and winter is cold but brightened by the Christmas market.

    Does Frankfurt get snow?

    Yes, Frankfurt gets snow in the winter, but it comes and goes and rarely stays long in the city streets. Frost and fog are more common than deep snow, and the river valley can hold cold air for days.

    How hot does Frankfurt get in summer?

    Summers in Frankfurt are warm, with long days and strong sun. Hot days can end with storms that bring heavy rain and lightning. The parks and the river banks give shade and cool air when the heat builds.
