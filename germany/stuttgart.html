    Stuttgart Germany Weather

    Welcome to the weather report for Stuttgart, in Baden-Württemberg, Germany (Coordinates: 48.7784°N, 9.1800°E; approximate elevation: 245 m). Stuttgart sits in a valley on the Neckar River, ringed by hills, woods, and vineyards. It is the capital of Baden-Württemberg and a city of cars, parks, and steep streets that climb out of the valley floor. The valley shapes the weather here. Cold air pools at the bottom on calm nights, fog gathers in the valley, and the hills above can sit in sun while the center stays grey. Stuttgart has a temperate climate, with warm summers and cold winters, and rain in every season. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather Stuttgart Germany

    Weather Stuttgart Germany is valley weather first and city weather second. In summer, the valley holds heat, and warm air builds through the day until a thunderstorm breaks it. The Neckar River adds a little moisture, and the vineyards on the slopes catch the sun. Because the city climbs steep hills, one part of Stuttgart can be clear while another sits in fog.
