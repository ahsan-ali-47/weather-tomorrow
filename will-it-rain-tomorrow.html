    Will It Rain Tomorrow

    Check will it rain tomorrow in Pakistan — live rain forecast for Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, powered by MET Norway.

    Live rain forecast for Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad — and how to read it.

    By Ahsan Ali, Founder, Weather Tomorrow · 19 November 2026

    Will It Rain Tomorrow in Pakistan?

    Will it rain tomorrow in Pakistan? It depends on where you are and what time of year it is. Rain in Pakistan is most likely during the monsoon, from July to September, and in winter, when western disturbances move across the country from the west. Outside those two seasons, rain tomorrow is unlikely in most cities — but a sudden weather system can still bring a shower. This page explains how to check the live rain forecast for tomorrow, how to read it, and what to do if rain is coming.

    The short answer is always the live forecast. Rain can start earlier or later than predicted, and a forecast made three days ago can shift. Check the forecast on the morning of your trip — the newest model run is always the most reliable.

    How to Check If It Will Rain Tomorrow

    Checking the rain forecast takes less than a minute:

    1. Open the page for your city. Use the Karachi weather, Lahore weather or Islamabad weather page — each one has a live forecast card, an hourly breakdown and a 10-day outlook for that city.

    2. Look at tomorrow in the 10-day outlook. Find tomorrow's row and read the weather symbol, the high and low temperatures, and the precipitation in millimetres.

    3. Read the symbol and the precipitation together. A rain symbol with a precipitation amount in millimetres means rain is expected tomorrow. A small number means light rain or a brief shower; a larger number means steadier or heavier rain.

    4. Check the hourly breakdown. It shows exactly which hours rain is expected, so you can plan to travel in the dry hours rather than during the rain.

    5. Check again in the morning. The forecast is recalculated every hour from fresh MET Norway model data, so the morning check is the one to trust.

    Will It Rain Tomorrow in Karachi?

    Karachi gets most of its rain during the monsoon, from July to September, when moist air from the Arabian Sea brings heavy showers. Outside the monsoon, rain in Karachi is rare — the city is hot and dry for most of the year. When rain does fall in Karachi, it can be heavy in a short time, and low-lying roads flood quickly. Check the live forecast on the Karachi weather page for tomorrow's rain, temperature and wind, and read the full guide in our blog post Is It Going to Rain Tomorrow?.

    Will It Rain Tomorrow in Lahore?

    Lahore gets rain in the monsoon, from July to September, and again in winter, when western disturbances arrive from December to February. Winter rain in Lahore is usually lighter and steadier than the monsoon downpours. Check the live forecast on the Lahore weather page: if tomorrow shows a rain symbol with precipitation in millimetres, rain is expected — and the hourly breakdown will show you when.

    Will It Rain Tomorrow in Islamabad?

    Islamabad gets more rain than Lahore and Karachi. It rains in the monsoon and in winter, and the city also gets pre-monsoon showers in June. Because Islamabad sits at the foot of the Margalla Hills, rain there can be heavier than in the plains, and it often arrives with thunder. Check the live forecast on the Islamabad weather page for tomorrow's rain, temperature and wind.

    How to Read the Rain Forecast

    The forecast gives you four things that matter for rain: the weather symbol, the temperature, the precipitation amount, and the wind. A rain symbol means the model expects rain. The temperature tells you whether it will fall as rain — above freezing means rain, near or below freezing in the mountains means snow or sleet instead. The precipitation amount, in millimetres, tells you how much is coming: a small number means a light shower, a larger number means steadier or heavier rain. Wind matters because strong wind with rain means the rain will feel heavier, and it can blow umbrellas inside out.

    The forecast for tomorrow is the most reliable part of the 10-day outlook. The first three to five days carry the highest confidence, while days six to ten come from ensemble model runs that are refreshed hourly.

    Rain, Drizzle and Showers — What Is the Difference?

    Rain is steady precipitation with drops big enough to feel. Drizzle is very light rain with tiny drops that fall slowly and barely wet the ground. Showers are short bursts of rain with dry gaps in between — one street can be wet while the next stays dry. On the forecast, a rain symbol with a small precipitation amount usually means light rain or showers; a larger amount means steadier or heavier rain. Thunderstorms are showers with lightning and thunder, and they can bring a lot of rain in a very short time, which is why roads flood so fast in Pakistani cities.

    Will It Be Raining Tomorrow All Day?

    Usually not. Most rain in Pakistan falls in bursts rather than all day. The monsoon can bring a long spell of rain, but even then there are breaks. The hourly breakdown on your city's weather page shows exactly which hours rain is expected tomorrow, so you can plan around the dry hours. If the hourly forecast shows rain only in the afternoon, you can do your morning work as normal and carry an umbrella for the afternoon.

    What to Do If Rain Is Forecast Tomorrow

    If rain is forecast tomorrow, carry an umbrella and a waterproof jacket, and wear shoes with grip — wet roads in Pakistani cities get slippery. Allow extra time for travel, because traffic slows down and low-lying roads flood in heavy rain. Keep your phone charged so you can check the hourly forecast, and avoid underpasses and low-lying roads that flood first. If you are riding a motorcycle, a raincoat is worth more than an umbrella. And if the forecast shows heavy rain with thunder, stay indoors during the storm — lightning is a real risk in the open.

    Will It Rain Tomorrow — The Bottom Line

    Will it rain tomorrow in Pakistan? Check the live forecast — that is the only answer that matters. Open the Karachi weather, Lahore weather or Islamabad weather page, look at tomorrow in the 10-day outlook, and read the rain symbol together with the precipitation amount. Check again in the morning, plan around the dry hours, and carry an umbrella if rain is coming.
