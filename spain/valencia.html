    Weather In Valencia In Spain

    Welcome to the weather report for Valencia, in the Valencian Community, Spain (Coordinates: 39.4697°N, 0.3763°W; approximate elevation: 15 m). Valencia sits on the east coast of Spain, on the Mediterranean Sea, where the Turia riverbed gardens run through the city to the sea. It is the third-largest city in Spain, a port with a long beach, a historic center, and the white shapes of the City of Arts and Sciences near the sea. The sea shapes the weather here. It keeps winters mild and brings a breeze on hot days, while the land inland heats up fast. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather Of Valencia Spain

    The weather of Valencia Spain is set by the sea and the sun. The Mediterranean holds warmth through autumn, so the air stays mild long after summer ends. In summer, the sea breeze cools the beach and the sea-front in the afternoon, while streets inland stay hotter. In winter, the sea keeps hard frost away, and cold days are short. Wind matters here. An east wind off the sea brings cloud and rain, while a west wind off the land brings dry, warm air.     Weather Valencia Spain

    Weather Valencia Spain in summer is hot, dry, and bright, with strong sun and warm nights. Humidity rises when the wind comes off the warm sea, so hot days can feel sticky near the beach. The sea breeze helps in the afternoon, most of all along the sand and the promenade. Inland, the air moves less and feels hotter. Thunderstorms are rare in high summer but can appear in late summer, with short, heavy rain. Drink water, stay in the shade at midday, and plan walks and beach time for the morning or the evening. The hourly page shows the hottest hours.

    Weather In Valencia Spain

    The weather in Valencia Spain in winter is mild, with sunny days, cool nights, and rain at times. Hard frost is rare, and snow is almost never seen in the city. Rain comes with east winds off the sea, and it can fall hard for a short time. Between rainy spells, winter days are often bright and pleasant, good for walks in the Turia gardens and the old town. A light warm coat is enough on most days, with an umbrella kept close. The 7-day strip shows which days look wet, and the hourly page shows when the rain should start and stop.

    Weather Valencia

    Weather Valencia in spring is mild and bright, with longer days and warmer air. March brings the Fallas festival, when the city fills with people, music, and fireworks, and the weather is usually kind, with sun and soft air. Showers pass through in spring, and the wind can change fast, but the rain rarely lasts all day. Orange trees bloom in spring, and their scent fills the streets. Layers are the smart choice, with a light coat for the evening. Spring is one of the best times to visit, before the heat of summer arrives.

    Spain Valencia Weather

    Spain Valencia weather in autumn stays warm at first, because the sea is still warm after summer. September and October can feel like summer, with warm days and mild nights. As autumn goes on, the risk of very heavy rain rises. Short, intense rain can fall when warm, wet air meets cooler air over the land, and streets can flood fast in a hard burst. Check the forecast on autumn mornings, and watch the hourly page when dark cloud builds. By late autumn, days turn cooler, rain is more common, and the sea breeze feels fresh.

    Weather In Valencia

    The weather in Valencia follows the rhythm of the sea breeze. By day, air moves in from the sea and cools the coast. At night, the flow can turn, and air drifts out from the land. This daily pattern shapes the feel of the city, most of all in summer, when the afternoon breeze is the best part of the day. The Turia gardens, which run through the city in the old riverbed, stay a little cooler than the streets around them, with shade and grass. The beach is windiest of all, so a calm morning there can turn breezy by the afternoon. The hourly page shows the wind hour by hour.

    December Weather In Valencia Spain

    December weather in Valencia Spain is mild, with sunny days and cool nights. Daytime air is often pleasant enough for a walk in the sun, while nights call for a warm coat. Rain is possible, and east winds can bring grey, wet days, but long cold spells are rare. The sea is still mild after autumn, which softens the coldest nights. Days are short, and the sun sets early, but clear December days are bright and fine. The city is calm in December, with few crowds, and the forecast on this page is the best guide for picking a dry day out.

    Weather In Valencia Spain In April

    The weather in Valencia Spain in April is warm and sunny, with showers that pass fast. Days are long, the sun is strong, and the air feels like early summer on fine days. Mornings can still be cool, so a light layer helps. April is a lovely month for the beach promenade, the gardens, and day trips along the coast, with fewer crowds than summer. Orange blossom scents the air in spring. Rain is possible, so keep an eye on the 7-day strip, and use the hourly page to find the driest hours for time outside.
