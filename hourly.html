      The Value of Hourly Precision in Weather Forecasting

      Micro-scale atmospheric fluctuations occur rapidly throughout the diurnal cycle. Morning surface inversions burn off under solar radiation, convective thunderstorms develop during late afternoons in monsoon months, and sudden wind shifts alter temperature and humidity within minutes.
