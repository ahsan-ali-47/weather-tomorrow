    Snowfall in Murree

    When Murree gets snow, the best time to visit, and how to plan a safe snow trip.

    By Ahsan Ali, Founder, Weather Tomorrow · 9 November 2026

    What Is Snowfall in Murree Like?

    Murree is a hill station in the Punjab hills, about 2,291 metres above sea level. In winter it turns white: snow covers the rooftops, the pine trees and the Mall Road, and night temperatures fall below freezing. For most people in Pakistan, Murree is the nearest place to see real snow, which is why thousands of visitors drive up from Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore every winter.

    Snowfall in Murree is not guaranteed on any single day. It comes in spells, usually with a winter storm, and a good snowfall can cover the town in a few hours. This guide explains when Murree gets snow, the best time to visit, and how to plan a safe trip.

    When Does It Snow in Murree?

    Snow in Murree usually falls from late December to February, with January the most likely month. The snow arrives with winter western disturbances, the storm systems that bring rain and snow to northern Pakistan in winter. A strong disturbance can bring heavy snow, while a weak one may bring only rain or a light dusting.

    It rarely snows in Murree in November or March, although a very cold spell can bring a light fall at the edges of the season. The exact dates change every year, so the snowfall forecast matters more than the calendar: if rain is forecast while temperatures are near or below freezing, snow is likely.

    The Best Time to Visit Murree for Snowfall

    January is the best time to visit Murree for snowfall, because that is when snow is most likely and the town is at its whitest. Late December and early February are also good, especially just after a winter storm has passed.

    Check the Murree weather page and the 10-day forecast a few days before you travel. If the forecast shows a snow symbol, plan to arrive before the storm, not during it, because roads can close while it is snowing. Weekends and public holidays are very crowded in Murree in winter, so a weekday trip is calmer and cheaper.

    How to Check Snowfall in Murree Before You Go

    The simplest way to check snowfall in Murree is the live forecast. On Weather Tomorrow, open the Murree weather page for today's conditions and the 10-day forecast for the days ahead. Look for the snow symbol and the temperature: a snow symbol with temperatures near or below freezing means snow is expected, while rain at the same temperature usually means sleet or snow on the higher ground around the town.

    Check again on the morning of your trip, because mountain weather changes fast. A forecast made three days ago can shift, and the newest model run is always the most reliable.

    Travel Tips for a Snow Trip to Murree

    A snow trip to Murree is easy to enjoy if you prepare for the cold and the road:

    Check the road before leaving. Roads to Murree can close in heavy snow. Confirm the road is open before you leave home, and do not travel during a snowstorm.

    Keep the car ready. Keep the fuel tank full, and carry warm clothes, food and water in the car in case of delays on the road.

    Drive slowly. Snow and ice make roads slippery. Drive slowly, keep a long gap from the car in front, and avoid sudden braking.

    Dress for real cold. Wear warm layers, a waterproof jacket, waterproof shoes, gloves and a warm cap. Nights fall below freezing, and standing in snow in light clothes quickly becomes miserable.

    Book your hotel in advance. In January, hotels in Murree fill fast, especially on weekends. Book before you travel instead of searching for a room after dark in the snow.

    Keep your phone charged. Cold drains batteries faster, and you will need your phone for the forecast, maps and calls.

    Snowfall in Murree — The Bottom Line

    Snowfall in Murree usually runs from late December to February, with January the best month to visit. The snow comes with winter storms, so check the live forecast before you travel, arrive before the storm rather than during it, and pack for real cold. Check the Murree weather today and the 10-day forecast before you leave, and the snow will be waiting for you instead of catching you by surprise.
