    Will It Snow Tomorrow

    Check will it snow tomorrow in Pakistan — live snow forecast for Murree, Skardu and Naran, powered by MET Norway.

    Live snow forecast for Murree, Skardu and Naran — and how to read it.

    By Ahsan Ali, Founder, Weather Tomorrow · 10 November 2026

    Will It Snow Tomorrow in Pakistan?

    Will it snow tomorrow in Pakistan? For most of the country the answer is no — snow falls only in the northern mountains. The places where snow is possible tomorrow are the hill towns: Murree, Skardu, Naran and the higher valleys of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan. This page explains how to check the live snow forecast for tomorrow, how to read it, and what to do if snow is coming.

    The short answer is always the live forecast. Mountain weather changes fast, and a forecast made three days ago can shift. Check the forecast on the morning of your trip — the newest model run is always the most reliable.

    How to Check If It Will Snow Tomorrow

    Checking the snow forecast takes less than a minute:

    1. Open the page for the place you are going to. Use the Snowfall in Murree, Snowfall in Skardu or Snowfall in Naran page — each one has a live forecast card, an hourly breakdown and a 10-day outlook for that town.

    2. Look at tomorrow in the 10-day outlook. Find tomorrow's row and read the weather symbol, the high and low temperatures, and the precipitation in millimetres.

    3. Read the symbol and the temperature together. A snow symbol with temperatures near or below freezing means snow is expected tomorrow. Rain at the same temperature usually means sleet, or snow only on the higher ground around the town.

    4. Check the hourly breakdown. It shows exactly which hours snow is expected, so you can plan to arrive before the snow starts rather than during it.

    5. Check again in the morning. The forecast is recalculated every hour from fresh MET Norway model data, so the morning check is the one to trust.

    Will It Snow Tomorrow in Murree?

    Murree is the nearest snow to Islamabad, about an hour's drive away, and it is where most Pakistanis go to see snow. Snow in Murree usually falls from late December to February, with January the most likely month. Outside that season, snow tomorrow is unlikely — but a very cold spell can bring a light fall at the edges of the season. Check the live forecast on the Snowfall in Murree page for tomorrow's snow, temperature and wind, and read the full guide in our blog post Snowfall in Murree — When It Snows & Best Time to Visit.

    Will It Snow Tomorrow in Skardu?

    Skardu is much colder than Murree, so precipitation in winter there is very likely to fall as snow. Snow in Skardu usually falls from December to February, and the town can stay snow-covered into March. Check the live forecast on the Snowfall in Skardu page: if tomorrow shows a snow symbol, plan to stay in town rather than travel, and expect the roads to be slow.

    Will It Snow Tomorrow in Naran?

    Naran sits at about 2,409 metres in the Kaghan Valley, so it is colder than Murree and snow arrives earlier — usually from late November — and stays longer, often into March. Check the live forecast on the Snowfall in Naran page. If snow is forecast tomorrow, confirm the valley road from Balakot is open before leaving, because it can close in heavy snow.

    How to Read the Snow Forecast

    The forecast gives you four things that matter for snow: the weather symbol, the temperature, the precipitation amount, and the wind. A snow symbol means the model expects snow. The temperature tells you whether it will stay as snow — near or below freezing means snow, above freezing usually means rain or sleet. The precipitation amount, in millimetres, tells you how much is coming: a small number means a light dusting, a larger number means steadier or heavier snow. Wind matters because strong wind with snow means drifting snow and poor visibility on the roads.

    The forecast for tomorrow is the most reliable part of the 10-day outlook. The first three to five days carry the highest confidence, while days six to ten come from ensemble model runs that are refreshed hourly.

    Snow, Sleet and Rain — What Is the Difference?

    Snow falls when the air is at or below freezing all the way from the cloud to the ground. Rain falls when the air is above freezing. Sleet is in between — snow that partly melts on the way down and arrives as icy pellets. In the mountains, the same storm can bring snow on the high ground and rain in the valley below, which is why the temperature on the forecast matters as much as the symbol. If the temperature is near freezing and the symbol shows rain, expect sleet or snow on the higher slopes around the town.

    Is Tomorrow a Snow Day in Pakistan?

    Pakistan has no official snow-day system like the United States, where a forecast can close schools in advance. When heavy snow falls, schools and offices in the affected hill towns decide locally whether to close, and the roads can close for safety. There is no single announcement to wait for: if snow is forecast tomorrow and you have to travel, check local news, confirm the road is open, and decide based on the forecast on this site. If you do not have to travel, the safest snow day is the one you spend indoors watching it fall.

    What to Do If Snow Is Forecast Tomorrow

    If snow is forecast tomorrow, plan to arrive before the snow starts, not during it. Confirm the road is open before leaving home. Keep the car fuelled, and carry warm clothes, food and water in case of delays. Book your hotel in advance — in January, hotels in the snow towns fill fast. Pack warm layers, a waterproof jacket, waterproof boots with grip, gloves and a warm cap, because nights in the snow towns fall below freezing. Drive slowly on snow and ice, keep a long gap from the car in front, and avoid sudden braking. Keep your phone charged — cold drains batteries faster, and you will need your phone for the forecast, maps and calls.

    Will It Snow Tomorrow — The Bottom Line

    Will it snow tomorrow in Pakistan? Only in the mountains — Murree, Skardu, Naran and the higher valleys. The way to know is the live forecast: open the Snowfall in Murree, Snowfall in Skardu or Snowfall in Naran page, look at tomorrow in the 10-day outlook, and read the snow symbol together with the temperature. Check again in the morning, arrive before the storm rather than during it, and pack for real cold.
