    Ottawa Weather Ontario

    Welcome to the weather report for Ottawa, in Ontario, Canada (Coordinates: 45.4209°N, 75.6901°W). Ottawa is the capital of Canada. It sits where the Ottawa River meets the Rideau River, with the city of Gatineau just across the Ottawa River in Quebec. Parliament Hill rises above the river in the heart of the city, and the Rideau Canal runs through the center on its way to Lake Ontario. Ottawa has a humid continental climate, which means cold, snowy winters and warm, humid summers, with clear springs and autumns in between. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Ottawa Ottawa Weather

    Ottawa Ottawa weather is shaped by rivers, flat land, and air that travels far. In winter, cold air from the north settles over the city, and the rivers freeze. In summer, warm, humid air moves up from the south, and the days feel heavy. The rivers add fog on calm mornings, most of all in autumn, when cool air sits over water that is still mild. Because the land is flat and open, wind can bite in winter and bring fast changes in every season. The ottawa weather ontario on this page follows all of this hour by hour, with live values from the MET Norway model for the exact spot of Ottawa.

    Weather In Canada Ottawa

    Weather in Canada Ottawa in winter is cold, snowy, and bright. Snow falls often from December to February, and it stays on the ground for weeks. Days are short, nights are long, and the air can be very cold when the wind picks up. In cold winters, the Rideau Canal freezes hard enough for people to skate on it, which is one of the best-known winter sights in the city. Dress for the feels-like temperature, not just the temperature, and cover skin on the coldest days. The 7-day strip shows which days look snowy, and the hourly page shows when snow is due to start and stop.

    Weather Of Ottawa Canada

    The weather of Ottawa Canada in spring is a season of thaw and change. Snow melts, the rivers rise, and rain replaces snow as the days warm. Mornings can still be frosty well into spring, while afternoons turn mild. Spring brings rain showers and the first warm days, and the city turns green fast once the thaw is done. Tulips bloom across the city in spring, in parks and along the canal. Layers are the smart choice, because a cold morning can end with a warm, sunny afternoon, and a wet day can follow a dry one with no warning.

    Weather Ottawa

    Weather Ottawa in summer is warm and humid, with sun, thunderstorms, and long days. Afternoon storms build fast, with heavy rain, lightning, and gusty wind, then clear just as fast. The air can feel sticky when humid air moves up from the south, and the warmest days call for shade, water, and a slower pace. Evenings by the river are often the best part of the day, with cooler air off the water. The hourly page shows the hottest hours and the timing of storms, so you can plan outdoor time around them.

    Weather Ottawa Ont

    Weather Ottawa Ont in autumn is cool and clear, with bright days and cold nights. The trees turn red and gold, and the air smells fresh after the first frost. Rain becomes more common as autumn goes on, and the first snow can arrive before the end of the season. Mornings bring fog near the rivers on calm days, which usually lifts by late morning. Autumn is a fine time for walks, with mild days and few crowds, but a warm coat is wise, because the gap between day and night is wide.
