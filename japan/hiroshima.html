    Japan Hiroshima Weather

    Welcome to the weather report for Hiroshima, in Hiroshima Prefecture, Japan (Coordinates: 34.3917°N, 132.4518°E). Hiroshima sits on the coast of the Seto Inland Sea, on the flat delta of the Ota River, with green hills rising around the city and the island of Miyajima a short ferry ride away. It is a modern city of rivers, bridges, parks, and trams, with a mild, sea-side climate. Hiroshima has a humid subtropical climate, which means hot, humid summers and mild winters, with a rainy season in early summer and clear, fresh air in autumn. This page shows live data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), so the forecast here comes from fresh model data, not guesswork.

    Weather Hiroshima Hiroshima Prefecture Japan

    Weather Hiroshima Hiroshima Prefecture Japan is set by the inland sea and the rivers. The Seto Inland Sea lies calm between the islands, and it keeps the air mild and moist. The Ota River splits into branches as it crosses the city, and bridges link the districts. Hills ring the delta, and they catch rain while the city center stays drier. In summer, warm, wet air moves in from the sea, and in winter, cold, dry air moves down from the north.     Weather In Hiroshima Japan

    The weather in Hiroshima Japan in summer is hot and humid, with a rainy season in June and July. Rain falls often in the rainy season, sometimes for days at a time, and the air stays warm and damp. After the rainy season, the heat builds, with strong sun and sticky nights. Thunderstorms appear at times, with heavy rain that clears fast. Drink water, stay in the shade at midday, and plan walks for the morning or the evening. The hourly page shows the hottest hours and the timing of rain, so you can plan the day around them.

    Weather In Hiroshima

    The weather in Hiroshima in late summer and autumn needs care, because typhoons can pass near the coast. A typhoon brings heavy rain, strong wind, and rough seas, and it can slow trains and ferries. The forecast shows the weather symbol for each day, so storm days stand out at a glance. For timing, the hourly page is the best view, because it shows when wind and rain are due to start, how long they may last, and when they should ease.     Weather Hiroshima

    Weather Hiroshima in winter is mild and dry, with cold nights and little snow in the city. Days are often bright and clear, good for walks along the rivers and visits to the parks. Snow is rare in the city center, though the hills around the city can see a light dusting. The air feels fresh and crisp, and the sea stays calm. A warm coat covers most winter days, with layers for the coldest mornings. Winter is a calm season in Hiroshima, with few crowds and clear views across the bay to Miyajima.

    Hiroshima Japan Weather

    Hiroshima Japan weather in spring is mild and bright, with cherry blossom in the parks and along the rivers. Days warm up fast, and the city turns pink and white for a short, lovely time. Rain showers pass through in spring, but they rarely last all day. The air feels fresh, and the hills turn green. Spring is one of the best times to visit, with easy weather for walks, the river paths, and day trips to Miyajima. Layers are the smart choice, with a light coat for the evening.
