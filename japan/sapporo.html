    Sapporo Weather Forecast

    Welcome to the weather page for Sapporo, in Hokkaido, Japan (Coordinates: 43.0618°N, 141.3545°E; elevation: 29 m). Sapporo is the largest city on Hokkaido, the northern island of Japan. A wide park, Odori Park, runs through the centre of the city from east to west, and the streets are laid out in a neat grid. The city is famous for its Snow Festival in winter, when great sculptures are built from snow and ice, and it once hosted the Winter Olympics. Ski hills rise close to the city, and the Sea of Japan lies a short way to the west. This page shows live weather data for the exact location of Sapporo, from the MET Norway weather model, updated every hour.

    Sapporo has a cold climate with snowy winters and short, mild summers. Winter brings deep snow, cold air from Siberia, and short days. Summer brings green parks, mild days, and cool nights, with rain at times. Spring and autumn are short and changeable. The sea to the west adds snow in winter, when cold air picks up dampness over the water and drops it as snow over the land.     Sapporo Weather

    Sapporo weather is ruled by the cold of the north. In the winter, cold air from Siberia flows over the Sea of Japan, picks up dampness, and falls as snow over the city. The snow can pile deep, and the streets are cleared through the winter. In the summer, the same sea brings mild air, and the days are warm but rarely hot. The mountains near the city catch more snow and more rain than the centre. The card at the top of this page shows the live temperature, wind, dampness, and pressure for Sapporo right now.

    Sapporo Japan Weather

    Sapporo Japan weather is far colder in winter than the weather in Tokyo or Osaka, which sit further south. While the south of Japan has mild winters, Sapporo has deep snow and freezing air for months. In the summer, the gap flips: Sapporo stays mild and fresh while the south grows hot and damp. That is why many people from the south visit Hokkaido in the summer, to enjoy the cool air, the flowers, and the green hills.     Hokkaido Sapporo Weather

    Hokkaido Sapporo weather fits the pattern of the whole island. The west coast, near the Sea of Japan, gets the deepest snow. The east coast gets less snow but colder air. The inland plains get frost and fog. Sapporo sits on the western side, in a plain ringed by hills, so it gets a great deal of snow and the full force of the winter storms.     Sapporo weather by month moves from deep winter to mild summer and back. The winter months are cold and snowy, with short days and long nights.
